About Us

Welcome to the Kenyans in Northern Territory Association

KINTA is a community association designed to bring together and support Kenyans living across the Northern Territory. Our purpose is to strengthen community bonds, promote Kenyan culture, provide practical support, and create opportunities for social and professional growth.

Whether you are newly arrived, a long-term resident, a student, a family, or a friend of the Kenyan community, this platform helps you connect, participate, and belong.
